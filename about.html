Our Story

Built Because the
Right Tool Didn't Exist.

ManSula BOS started from one frustrating reality: every POS Software, Billing Software, and Business Management Software in the Indian market was either too expensive, too complicated, or stopped working the moment the internet went down.

"Every POS Software product we tested required monthly subscriptions, broke during internet outages, and locked our data behind someone else's server. We needed a Business Operating System we could actually trust — one that worked offline, stored data locally, and cost nothing to run."
— ManSula DivLabs, Founding Team
The specific pain points that led to building ManSula BOS:
Existing Offline POS Software options were outdated, ugly, or Windows-only
Billing Software with cloud sync uploaded your sensitive sales data to external servers
Inventory Management Software required paid subscriptions just to track stock
No single Business Management Software combined POS + Udhaar + Analytics in one
Khata apps only tracked credit — not integrated with any Customer Management system

So we built ManSula BOS: a complete Business Operating System that is offline-first, free forever, stores all data on your device, and treats you as a professional — not a monthly revenue line item.

Our Mission

A Business Management Software That Respects You.

ManSula BOS is built on the belief that every small business owner in India — whether they run a cafe, a pharmacy, a fashion boutique, or a grocery store — deserves the same powerful Business Operating System that large enterprises use, without the price tag.

We built this as an Offline POS Software because we know that internet connectivity in India is unreliable. A Retail Management System that fails during peak hours is not a solution — it is a problem.

We built the Billing Software to be privacy-first because your daily revenue figures, your customer list, and your Udhaar ledger are sensitive business data. They should live on your device, not on someone else's cloud server.

We built the Business Analytics module because most shop owners make pricing and stocking decisions based on gut feel — when the right data could make those decisions obviously correct.

And we kept it free because the Inventory Management Software, Customer Management, and POS Software tools that improve your business should not themselves be a cost drain on that business.

Design Philosophy

Why Every Design Decision
in Our Business Operating System Matters.

Mobile-First POS Software

The majority of Indian shop owners use their phone as their primary device. Our POS Software and Inventory Management Software are designed for a 5-inch screen first — not a desktop with a tiny mobile mode tacked on.

Speed as a Core Feature

During lunch rush at a cafe or during sale season at a retail shop, every second counts. Our Offline POS Software and Billing Software are optimised for sub-second response times — even on low-end devices.

Built for South Asia

Support for Indian Rupee, GST tax slabs, UPI QR payments, Khata/Udhaar naming conventions, and the specific workflows of Indian Retail Management System and Restaurant POS environments — not a translated Western product.
