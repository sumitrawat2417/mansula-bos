1. Overview & Core Principle

ManSula BOS is an offline-first Business Operating System. Your business data — including all POS Software transactions, Inventory Management records, Customer Management profiles, Udhaar Khata ledger, and Business Analytics — is stored exclusively on your device using your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. We do not upload, transmit, or store your operational business data on any external server.

ManSula DivLabs ("we," "us," or "our") operates the ManSula BOS Business Operating System (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, use, and disclosure when you use our Application, and the choices you have associated with that information.

This policy applies to all users of ManSula BOS, including the POS Software module, Inventory Management Software module, Billing Software module, Customer Management CRM module, Business Analytics module, and all other components of the Business Operating System.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter Into the Application (Business Data)

The following data is entered by you into ManSula BOS and is stored only on your device:

  • Business name, address, GSTIN, and UPI payment ID (for Billing Software configuration)
  • Product catalogue, pricing, and cost data (for Inventory Management Software and POS Software)
  • Sales transactions and order history generated through the POS Software module
  • Inventory Tracking records, stock levels, and purchase entries
  • Customer Management profiles, including names, phone numbers, notes, and visit history
  • Udhaar Khata ledger entries, credit balances, and repayment records
  • Business Analytics data computed from your sales and inventory records

None of the above business data is transmitted to ManSula DivLabs or any third party. All computation — including all Business Analytics — is performed locally on your device.

2.2 Data We May Collect (Non-Business Data)

If you access our website (mansulanexus.app), we may collect the following non-personally-identifiable information through standard web analytics:

  • Anonymised page visit counts and navigation paths
  • Browser type and operating system (anonymised)
  • General geographic region (country-level only, not city or precise location)
  • Referrer URL (the page you came from before visiting our website)

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us.

3. How Data Is Stored

ManSula BOS is a Progressive Web Application (PWA) that stores all business operational data on your device using:

  • IndexedDB: Used to store your complete Inventory Management Software catalogue, Customer Management profiles, POS Software transaction history, and Udhaar Khata ledger entries
  • localStorage: Used to store application settings and configuration (business name, Billing Software settings, POS Software preferences)
  • Service Worker Cache: Used to cache the application code so the Offline POS Software works without internet connectivity

When you use the backup feature, your data is exported as an encrypted file and downloaded directly to your device's local storage. This backup file is never transmitted to or stored on ManSula DivLabs' servers.

Since all data is stored on your device, ManSula DivLabs has no technical ability to access, view, or recover your business data. If you uninstall the application or clear your browser data without creating a backup, your data cannot be recovered by us.

4. How Data Is Used

Your business data entered into the ManSula BOS Business Operating System is used solely for the purpose of operating the application features you use:

  • POS Software: Processing sales transactions and generating Billing Software receipts
  • Inventory Management Software: Deducting stock, tracking Inventory levels, computing margins
  • Business Analytics: Computing revenue, profit, trends, and sales reports from your transaction history
  • Customer Management: Building and displaying customer profiles linked to transaction history
  • Udhaar Khata: Recording and displaying credit balances and repayment history

We do not use your business data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the features of the Business Operating System on your device.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

ManSula BOS does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage and IndexedDB exclusively to make the Offline POS Software functional. These are not cookies and cannot be read by any third party.

Our website (mansulanexus.app) may use a minimal set of first-party cookies for:

  • Session management (ensuring the PWA install prompt functions correctly)
  • User preference storage (dark/light mode if implemented)

You can clear all application data at any time through your browser settings (Settings → Application → Storage → Clear Site Data). Note that this will delete all your Business Management Software data if you have not created a backup.

6. Third-Party Services

ManSula BOS does not integrate with any third-party data collection services within the application itself. However, the following third-party services may be used on our website:

  • Google Fonts: Used to load the Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans typefaces. Google may log a connection request from your IP address when fonts are fetched. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Web hosting provider: Our website hosting provider maintains standard server access logs as required for security purposes.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. Your Retail Management System data, Customer Management data, and all other business operational data remains exclusively on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

ManSula BOS Business Operating System is designed for business owners and operators. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 18. If you believe that a child under 18 has provided us with information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about what personal data we hold about you (note: we hold virtually none, as your business data stays on your device)
  • Correction: Request correction of any inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data from our systems
  • Portability: Export your business data at any time using the built-in backup feature in the Business Management Software
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Stop using the application at any time — your local data remains on your device until you clear it

To exercise any rights relating to data we may hold (e.g., contact form submissions), email us at privacy@mansulanexus.app.

9. Data Security

The security of your business data is paramount. We implement the following measures:

  • All business data (POS Software records, Inventory, Customer Management, Udhaar, Analytics) is stored locally on your device — the most secure form of data protection
  • Backup files exported from the Business Operating System are encrypted before download
  • No data transmission means no transmission-layer security risk for your operational data
  • The application is served over HTTPS at all times

Since we do not receive or store your business operational data, we cannot be subject to a data breach that exposes your sales records, customer profiles, or inventory data. This is the architectural security advantage of a locally-stored Offline POS Software and Business Operating System.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of ManSula BOS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Significant changes (e.g., if we ever introduce a cloud sync feature) will be prominently notified within the application itself before taking effect.
