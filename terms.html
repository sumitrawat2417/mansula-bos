2. Description of Service

ManSula BOS is a Progressive Web Application (PWA) that functions as an offline-first Business Operating System for small and medium enterprises. The Application includes the following modules and features:

  • POS Software / Point of Sale: Counter-mode Offline POS Software for processing sales, managing orders, and accepting payments including UPI, cash, and card
  • Inventory Management Software: Real-time Inventory Tracking, stock deduction on sales, low-stock alerts, and margin analysis
  • Billing Software: GST-compliant receipt generation with UPI QR codes, thermal printer support, and PDF/WhatsApp sharing
  • Business Analytics: On-device computation of revenue, profit, sales trends, peak hours, and payment method analysis
  • Customer Management CRM: Customer profiles, visit history, lifetime value, VIP tagging, and personal notes
  • Udhaar Khata Ledger: Digital credit tracking per customer with repayment history and total receivables
  • Data Backup and Restore: One-tap encrypted export and import of complete Business Operating System data

The Application is currently provided free of charge. We reserve the right to introduce optional premium features in the future, which will be clearly communicated to users before implementation.
